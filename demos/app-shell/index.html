Why this page exists

Layout contract

The shell is layout-agnostic: it owns no <html> and wraps no layout. Here <body> is the fixed-height parent (height: 100dvh), so the bar and the rail are pinned and only this region scrolls. The rail is a sibling of the bar rather than a region under it, which is what lets it run the full height.
